# Slides, Module 1: Foundations and Mindset

Track: Core, for everyone. Length: 1.5 hours.

How to read this file:

- S# marks a slide. On screen text is kept short on purpose. The slide supports you, it does not replace you.
- DEMO means switch to a screen recording, with no slide.
- Say is the key line to deliver over the slide.
- Visual is an image or icon suggestion for the designer.

Full talking scripts come later in `scripts/module-01.md`.

## Lesson 1.1: Welcome and What You Will Build (6 minutes)

- S1, Title. "Claude Zero to Master." Subtitle: chat, code, and the API. Visual: course logo and your name. Say: welcome, over the next twenty hours you go from complete beginner to confident user.
- S2, The promise. One line covering use, automate, build, earn. Say the full promise from the blueprint.
- S3, Who this is for. Icons for student, freelancer, business owner, developer. Say: whoever you are, there is a track for you here.
- S4, Course map. The single diagram showing the shared path that later branches into Builder and Developer.
- S5, What you will build. Three thumbnails of finished student projects.
- S6, Recap. These skills are already sold as small services online.

## Lesson 1.2: AI and Language Models in Plain English (10 minutes)

- S1, Title. "What is AI, without the fear."
- S2, The core idea. A language model is a very capable version of predictive text. Visual: a phone keyboard suggesting the next word.
- S3, Tokens. Words are broken into small pieces, and the model predicts what comes next. Visual: a sentence splitting into pieces.
- S4, Why it feels capable. It has read an enormous amount of text and learned the patterns.
- S5, What it is not. It is not a search engine and not a calculator. It is a reasoning partner.
- S6, A limitation to remember. It can be confidently wrong. We deal with that later.
- S7, Recap. Prediction, patterns, and a partner rather than an oracle.

## Lesson 1.3: What Claude Is and Why It Matters (8 minutes)

- S1, Title. "Claude and Anthropic."
- S2, Who makes it. Anthropic, a company with a strong focus on safety.
- S3, Strengths. Long documents, honesty, strong writing and coding.
- S4, A fair comparison. A neutral table comparing Claude with other popular assistants.
- S5, DEMO. Ask the same question and point out the careful, well reasoned answer.
- S6, Recap. Choosing the right tool for the job is itself a useful skill.

## Lesson 1.4: The Three Surfaces (8 minutes)

- S1, Title. "Three ways to use Claude."
- S2, The chat app. Have a conversation and get work done. For everyone.
- S3, Claude Code. Give instructions to your computer in the terminal. For builders.
- S4, The API. Put Claude inside your own software. For developers. Visual: three columns with icons and a label for who each is for.
- S5, Your path. Highlight which modules cover which surface.
- S6, Recap. Start with chat, then Claude Code, then the API.

## Lesson 1.5: Account Setup, Free, Pro, and Max (10 minutes)

- S1, Title. "Setting up your account."
- S2, Plan comparison. A table of Free, Pro, and Max, with a note to re check prices before recording.
- S3, Which to choose. Start on Free to learn, move to Pro for serious work.
- S4, DEMO. Sign up live on the web.
- S5, DEMO. Install the desktop app and the mobile app.
- S6, Practice. Create an account and install the app.

## Lesson 1.6: Your First Real Result in Ten Minutes (12 minutes)

- S1, Title. "Your first result."
- S2, Two quick wins. A polished email, and a summary of a PDF.
- S3, DEMO one. Turn a rough note into a professional email.
- S4, DEMO two. Upload a PDF and ask for a five point summary.
- S5, Practice. Try the same with your own file or email.
- S6, Recap. These two tasks alone are common freelance jobs.

## Lesson 1.7: Privacy, Data, and Responsible Use (10 minutes)

- S1, Title. "Using Claude safely."
- S2, Your data. How the training setting works and the privacy basics.
- S3, Simple rules. Do not paste passwords or secrets, and be careful with sensitive client data.
- S4, DEMO. A tour of the privacy and data settings.
- S5, Responsible use. Do not plagiarise, verify facts, and cite honestly.
- S6, Recap. Three safety rules to remember.

## Lesson 1.8: How to Get the Most From This Course (6 minutes)

- S1, Title. "Getting the most from this course."
- S2, The rule. Do not just watch. Pause and practise after every lesson.
- S3, Setup. Make a practice folder and a notes document.
- S4, Tracks. A reminder of Core for everyone and the optional Builder and Developer modules.
- S5, Project. Save screenshots of the three results you produced today.
- S6, A three question review and a link to the Module 1 summary.

### Designer notes

- Give each track a consistent colour so students recognise it instantly.
- Use the same title layout and the same recap layout in every lesson so the course looks consistent.
- Keep on screen text to roughly six words per line.
