# Slides, Module 4: Real World Use Cases Across Industries

Track: Core, for everyone. Length: 3 hours.

Each lesson follows the same rhythm: a hook, a process slide, a demonstration, a practice task, then a recap with an earning note.

Labels: S# for a slide, DEMO for a screen recording, Say for the key line, Visual for a design suggestion.

## Lesson 4.1: Business and Operations (16 minutes)

- S1, Title. "Run a business faster."
- S2, Use cases. Standard procedures, emails, meeting notes into actions, and strategy drafts.
- S3, The process. Rough input, Claude, clean output.
- S4, DEMO. Rough notes into a standard procedure, and a meeting transcript into action items.
- S5, Practice. Turn your own notes into a procedure.
- S6, Recap. Sell operations setup to local businesses.

## Lesson 4.2: Marketing and Content (16 minutes)

- S1, Title. "A content engine."
- S2, Use cases. Content calendars, ad copy, search, and repurposing.
- S3, DEMO. One article turned into ten social posts with captions.
- S4, DEMO. Keywords into an outline into a draft.
- S5, Practice. Repurpose one piece of content.
- S6, Recap. Content creation is steady freelance work.

## Lesson 4.3: Sales and Outreach (14 minutes)

- S1, Title. "Sales that converts."
- S2, Use cases. Cold emails, proposals, follow ups, and notes.
- S3, DEMO. A batch of personalised cold emails from a list of leads.
- S4, Practice. Write a proposal for a real prospect.
- S5, Recap. Offer outreach and proposal writing as a service.

## Lesson 4.4: Freelancing on Fiverr and Upwork (18 minutes)

- S1, Title. "Freelancing, done faster."
- S2, Where Claude helps. Gig descriptions, proposals, delivery speed, and client messages.
- S3, DEMO. A strong gig description and a proposal.
- S4, DEMO. Deliver a sample order, such as five product descriptions, in minutes.
- S5, Pricing. What to charge and how to scale.
- S6, Practice. Create one gig and one proposal.
- S7, Recap. This lesson alone can produce a first income.

## Lesson 4.5: Students and Researchers (16 minutes)

- S1, Title. "Study smarter, do not cheat."
- S2, Use cases. Summaries, study plans, explaining hard topics, and thesis structure.
- S3, DEMO. Summarise a chapter and make a study plan.
- S4, Ethics. Learn from it, do not plagiarise, and cite honestly.
- S5, Practice. Summarise a topic and quiz yourself.
- S6, Recap. Offer tutoring and study materials.

## Lesson 4.6: Finance and Data Analysis (16 minutes)

- S1, Title. "From data to insight."

- S6, Recap. Data cleaning and reporting are common gigs.

## Lesson 4.7: Human Resources and Recruiting (12 minutes)

## Lesson 4.8: Legal and Contracts, Used With Care (12 minutes)
