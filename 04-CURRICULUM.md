# Curriculum — Modules divided into lessons

Each module is divided into lessons. A lesson is one recordable video with its own objective, length, and slides. This is the structure for the course platform and for recording, record one lesson at a time.

---

## Module 1: Get Started with Claude (6 lessons, about 1.5 hours)

**Lesson 1.1 — Meet Claude** · about 7 minutes
- Objective: explain what Claude is and who makes it, and why it is a thinking partner, not a chatbot.
- Slides: Who makes Claude (Anthropic), More than a chatbot.
- Practice: list three tasks you want to try.

**Lesson 1.2 — What Claude Can Do** · about 9 minutes
- Objective: see Claude handle a real task end to end.
- Slides: What can Claude do for you, Plan a weekend trip to Lahore.
- Demo: plan the Lahore trip live.
- Practice: repeat with your own trip.

**Lesson 1.3 — The Four Habits** · about 8 minutes
- Objective: learn the 4D mindset for working with AI, and see it fits any job.
- Slides: How to work with your thinking partner (Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence), The four habits fit any job.
- Practice: apply the four habits to one task.

**Lesson 1.4 — Get Set Up** · about 7 minutes
- Objective: create an account and pick the right plan.
- Slides: How to get Claude, Free, Pro, or Max.
- Demo: live sign up and app install.
- Practice: create an account.

**Lesson 1.5 — Your First Results** · about 10 minutes
- Objective: complete a first real task, and learn to get much better answers.
- Slides: Your first real task, Get much better answers.
- Practice: do one tiny task, then improve a weak answer by adding context.

**Lesson 1.6 — Use It Responsibly and Wrap** · about 6 minutes
- Objective: use Claude safely, reflect, and start the module project.
- Slides: Using Claude responsibly, Practice and what is next, Module 1 project (plan your own trip).
- Project: plan your own trip with all four habits.

---

## Module 2: Master the Claude App (7 lessons, about 2.5 hours)

**Lesson 2.1 — The App and Your Conversations** · about 10 minutes
- Objective: navigate the app, and control a conversation with edit, branch, and retry.
- Slides: A quick tour of the app, Manage your conversations.
- Practice: turn on web search, and branch a conversation by editing a message.

**Lesson 2.2 — Files, Models, and Tools** · about 15 minutes
- Objective: feed Claude files, choose the right model and effort, and pick the right tool.
- Slides: Files and images, Choose your model and effort, Search think or research.
- Habits: Discernment, Delegation.
- Practice: summarise a PDF; compare a fast and a deep model; pick the tool for three tasks.

**Lesson 2.3 — Build with Artifacts** · about 15 minutes
- Objective: understand what an Artifact is and build and share one.
- Slides: Build it do not just chat about it (What, Why, When, How), Things you can build.
- Demo: live build a study tracker with a progress bar, then publish.
- Practice: build your own study tracker.

**Lesson 2.4 — Set Up a Project** · about 14 minutes
- Objective: create a Project with instructions and a knowledge base, and understand how context works.
- Slides: Your reusable workspace (What, Why, When), Projects worth setting up.
- Habit: Description.
- Demo: live build the Final Exam Prep Project, and show the before and after.
- Practice: create your Project and set its instructions.

**Lesson 2.5 — Memory** · about 8 minutes
- Objective: use the three layers of memory correctly.
- Slides: Memory in three layers.
- Practice: add a memory and confirm it in Settings.

**Lesson 2.6 — Power Features** · about 10 minutes
- Objective: use Claude in Chrome and Scheduled tasks.
- Slides: Claude in Chrome, Scheduled tasks.
- Practice: summarise a web page in Chrome; set a daily brief task.

**Lesson 2.7 — Your Pro Workflow and Capstone** · about 10 minutes
- Objective: apply a repeatable decision workflow, answer common questions, and finish the capstone.
- Slides: Your quick decision guide, Common questions, Module 2 project.
- Project: finish your exam prep workspace (instructions, knowledge base, Memory, and your Artifact).

## How this maps to the deck
The deck slides stay as they are. This curriculum groups them into lessons. When recording, start a new video at each lesson, use that lesson's slides, run the demo, and end with the practice.

Optional next step: add a short lesson title card slide before each lesson in the deck, so each recorded video opens with its own title.
