## AI Fluency definition
The ability to work with AI in ways that are **effective, efficient, ethical, and safe.**

## The three interaction modes (base layer)
- **Automation** AI executes a defined task from your prompt (emails, summaries, code)
- **Augmentation** human and AI co-define and co-execute as partners (essays, complex work)
- **Agency** you configure an AI system to act on its own for others (tutors, chatbots, characters)

## The 4D Framework (four interconnected competencies, NOT sequential steps)
Exact definitions (quote-worthy):
- **Delegation** setting goals and deciding whether, when, and how to engage AI. Sub-parts: Problem/Goal Awareness, Platform Awareness, Task Delegation.
- **Description** effectively describing goals to prompt useful AI behaviour and outputs. Sub-parts: Product Description (what the output is), Process Description (the iterative dialogue), Performance Description (how an AI system behaves for users).
- **Discernment** accurately assessing the usefulness of AI outputs and behaviour. Sub-parts: Product Discernment (output quality), Process Discernment (is the collaboration working), Performance Discernment (test user-facing systems).
- **Diligence** taking responsibility for what we do with AI and how. Sub-parts: Creation Diligence (ethics, bias, stakeholder impact), Transparency Diligence (disclose AI use), Deployment Diligence (fact-check, test, own what you ship).

## The loops (signature diagrams)
- **Description ↔ Discernment loop** the engine of Augmentation: describe, AI produces, you discern, you refine your description, repeat. The most cited diagram.
- **Delegation ↔ Diligence loop** (from the educators' course, worth adopting): the bookends of a project, delegate at the start, be diligent at the end.

## Six prompting techniques (from Lesson 7, quote-worthy)
1. Give context, be specific about what you want, why, and background
2. Show examples of the output style or format
3. Specify constraints, format, length, requirements
4. Break complex tasks into steps
5. Ask the AI to think first, give it space to work through its process
6. Define the AI's role or tone
Plus the "secret weapon": ask the AI itself to help refine your prompt. Prompting is iterative.

## Recurring worked scenarios (reuse the SAME three across all four Ds)
Marketing email, a data/spreadsheet analysis, a story character. (For our global no-code audience, keep the email and the character, swap the research dataset for a spreadsheet or planning task.)

## Signature exercises
- **Plan a real project** and make delegation choices, then SAVE it and reuse it for Description, Discernment, Diligence (the course's through-line project).
- **Bad Prompt Makeover** (quick, high engagement).
- Open a model and practise (Lesson 1).
