# Module 1 enrichment — Claude 101 content (in our words)

Substance from Anthropic's own help center and docs, to make Module 1 lessons richer. Rewrite in our voice, do not copy verbatim. Verify plan-gated facts before recording.

## 1.1 What is Claude?
- Claude is a large language model built by Anthropic, trained to be a helpful, honest, and harmless assistant with a conversational tone.
- Access on web (claude.ai), desktop (Mac and Windows), and mobile (iOS and Android). Must be 18+ and in a supported region.
- A prompt is simply your message to Claude. Talk to it like a coworker or a friend, naturally and conversationally.
- Prompts range from a simple question ("how do I hang a picture frame") to a complex multi-step request.
- Strengths: writing, analysis, coding, planning, creative work; strongest in English, good in many languages.

## 1.2 Your first conversation (the four-beat arc)
1. Start simple: begin with straightforward questions to learn how Claude responds.
2. Be specific: the more specific your request, the better Claude can help.
3. Iterate: refine your prompt or ask follow ups, do not expect a perfect result on the first try.
4. Explore: test Claude across different kinds of tasks.
- Interface: type a message and send; more options via the plus button or by typing a slash.

## 1.3 Getting better results
- The golden rule: show your prompt to a colleague who knows nothing about the task. If they would be confused, Claude will be too.
- The more precisely you explain what you want, the better the result. Ambiguous wording is the leading cause of weak answers.
- Give context and motivation: telling Claude why you want something helps it hit the target.
- Use numbered steps when order or completeness matters.
- Warning: longer prompts are not automatically better. Start simple, add detail only when it helps, and test each change.

## 1.6 Introduction to Projects
- A Project is a self-contained workspace with its own chats and a knowledge base.
- Knowledge base: upload documents, text, or code that Claude uses across every chat in that project.
- Project instructions: set how Claude should behave for the whole project (tone, role, format).
- The rule people miss: context is not shared across chats in a project unless it is in the knowledge base.
- How to: claude.ai/projects, New Project, add name and description, add knowledge, set instructions, save. Star to pin, archive to keep.
- Verify: free plan caps at five projects; paid plans add automatic retrieval as knowledge grows.

## 1.7 Creating with Artifacts
- An Artifact is substantial, standalone content Claude puts in a dedicated window next to the chat.
- Claude makes one when the content is significant and self-contained (typically over fifteen lines), something you will edit, reuse, or refer back to.
- Types: documents, code, single-page sites, images and diagrams, and interactive tools.
- The workflow that works: share your idea and your problem, let Claude ask you clarifying questions, then say "create this for me", then iterate.
- Fun example prompts: a wedding planner bot that knows your vendor details; a game where kids battle monsters by solving maths problems; a small-business tool.
- Publish gives a shareable link; viewers can interact, or make their own copy to modify.
- Verify: turn on "Code execution and file creation" in Settings, Capabilities.

## Adopt across Module 1
- Open with the "coworker or friend" analogy.
- Make "start simple, be specific, iterate, explore" the spine of the first conversation lesson.
- Make the "show your prompt to a colleague" golden rule the centerpiece of getting better results.
- Foreground the "context is not shared unless in the knowledge base" rule for Projects.
- Teach the artifact creation criteria and the "let Claude interview you" workflow, with the playful examples.
