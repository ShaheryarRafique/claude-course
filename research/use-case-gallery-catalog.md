# Claude use-case gallery — catalog for mapping examples

Source: https://claude.com/resources/use-cases (6 pages, ~90 use cases). Use these real, Anthropic-published use cases as the source of examples across the course, so every task we teach is one Claude genuinely does well.

Key rule: the newest "wow" use cases (Cowork, Claude for Excel, Claude in Chrome, connectors) need setup and are NOT beginner single-chat. The beginner-safe ones run on plain claude.ai with a prompt and maybe one file. Save the advanced ones for Modules 3 to 5.

Record-time caveats: do not hardcode a model name (say "the current default model"); confirm the free tier still allows Artifacts and image upload before recording, since the interactive-visual and recipe-card wins depend on them.

## Best beginner "first conversation" tasks (single chat, quick win, relatable to South Asian students and professionals)
1. Explain a hard concept with an interactive visual (physics, stats, economics). Student wow.
2. Learn a formula by dragging data points (stats, exam prep). No file needed.
3. Thoughtful gift on a budget ("a gift for my sister who loves art, under 2000 rupees, available locally").
4. Daily travel itinerary ("plan a 2 day Jaipur/Lahore trip on a student budget").
5. Handwritten recipe or notes to a clean digital card (upload one photo, shows image reading).
6. Draft an email or message (leave request, cover letter, client follow-up) in polished English.
7. Plan your career path / skills gap ("I know Excel and basic Python, I want a data analyst job, what next?").
8. Practice a job or case interview with feedback (mock interview in one chat, great before placements).
9. Create a process flowchart or a one-page brand asset (flyer for a tuition centre or small shop).
10. Chart a small CSV of marks or sales and get a plain-English reading.
11. Organize simple business finances for a shopkeeper or freelancer.
12. Explain a rental or job-offer document in plain English (caution: verify with a professional).

## By role (beginner-friendly unless marked ADVANCED)
- Student: interactive concept visual; learn a formula live; chart your data; map your gaps into a mini-lesson; case-interview practice; debate practice; career path; notes/equations to LaTeX (needs a photo).
- Teacher: adapt a textbook page to reading levels; whiteboard sketch to interactive visual; syllabus dependency map; interactive diagram tools.
- Researcher: map a lit review; plan a lit review; research to slides; verify statistics from data; voice-note ideas to research; grant options scatter.
- Marketing/design: brand assets (flyers, cards); adapt content across platforms; customer personas; campaign performance (needs data). ADVANCED: audit an asset folder, brand-guideline Skill.
- Sales: sales proposal deck (beginner). ADVANCED: battle cards, sales reports, CRM logging.
- Finance: organize business finances; understand an inherited spreadsheet; stress-test a financial plan. ADVANCED: financial models, credit/investment memos, model updates (Claude for Excel + connectors).
- HR: new-hire onboarding guides.
- Legal: contract redlining in plain English (paste/upload). ADVANCED: discovery timelines, compliance prep.
- Business owner / ops: process flowchart; interactive PDF forms; company newsletter; compare vendor options; market sizing. ADVANCED: Cowork desktop/Drive automation, daily briefing, dashboard metrics.
- Personal (great relatable material): gift giving; travel itinerary; compare destinations; digital recipe cards; health/exercise notes; bucket-list app; portfolio webpage.

## By task type
- Write: emails, onboarding guides, newsletter, impact report, sales deck, AI policy.
- Summarise: feedback themes, lit review, notes to key points.
- Analyse data: chart a CSV, campaign performance, verify statistics, organize finances, inherited spreadsheet.
- Plan/decide: syllabus, career path, lit review, trip itinerary, budget futures, theory of change.
- Research: compare options, compare products across sites, size a market, compare destinations.
- Build a tool (Artifacts): interactive diagram, bucket-list app, foraging guide, webpage, formula explainer, PDF form, flowchart.
- Learn/study: concept visual, formula live, gap mini-lesson, interview practice, debate practice.
- Code/automate: mostly ADVANCED (Cowork, connectors) for Modules 3 to 5.
