# Anthropic Academy Blueprint

Strategy: mirror the structure of Anthropic's own Academy (Skilljar) courses across our whole course. Follow Claude 101 first, then their other courses for the deeper modules. We follow their structure and topic order, take definitions from their docs, and add our tailoring (local examples, plain English) and the earning angle they do not cover. We do not copy their content.

## Master mapping: Anthropic course to our module

- **Module 1: Get Started with Claude** mirrors Claude 101, section "Meet Claude":
  1.1 What is Claude? · 1.2 Your first conversation · 1.3 Getting better results · 1.4 How you'll work with Claude
- **Module 2: Master the Claude App** mirrors Claude 101, section "Organizing your work and knowledge":
  Introduction to projects · Creating with artifacts · Memory · Other ways to work (Chrome, scheduled tasks). (Skills moves to Module 5.)
- **Module 3: Prompt Like a Pro** builds on Claude 101 "Getting better results", plus our own depth.
- **Module 4: Put Claude to Work** mirrors Claude 101, section "Putting it all together": Claude in action, use-cases by role.
- **Module 5: Automate with Claude Cowork** mirrors Claude 101, section "Expanding Claude's reach" (Connecting your tools, Enterprise search, Research for deep dives), plus Introduction to Agent Skills, Introduction to MCP, and Introduction to Claude Cowork.
- **Module 6: Build with Claude Code** mirrors Claude Code 101, Claude Code in Action, and Introduction to Subagents.
- **Module 7: Build Real AI Apps** mirrors Building with the Claude API, and MCP Advanced Topics.
- **Module 8: Ship and Earn** mirrors the Conclusion and certificate pattern, plus our own earning and freelancing content.

Foundations: the AI Fluency framework (the four habits, our 4D backbone) runs through every module.

## How we mirror a course
For each Anthropic course, read its lesson list and per-lesson pattern (estimated time, learning objectives, key takeaways, an interactive moment, a reflection, and what's next). Group our slides into the same lessons and order, then layer our local examples and earning notes on top. Definitions and limits come from support.claude.com and platform.claude.com.
