# Opening Script — Cover, Instructor, Roadmap

The first two to three minutes of the course. Read it calmly and with quiet confidence. Look into the camera, slow down on the key lines, and let the numbers breathe. Plain professional English, no hype. Slides referenced are the first three: the cover, your instructor slide, and the course roadmap.

---

## Slide 1 — Cover ("Claude Zero to Master")

> "Assalam o alaikum, and welcome to Claude Zero to Master.
>
> Over the next twenty hours, you go from complete beginner to genuinely capable. Not by watching, by doing.
>
> Here is the promise. By the end, you will use Claude for real work, automate the parts of your day you would rather not do, build your own tools with no coding, and know how to turn these skills into income.
>
> No hype, and no filler. Every lesson is a skill you can use the same day. Student, professional, business owner, or developer, there is a clear path here for you.
>
> Let us begin."

Delivery notes: this is the first impression, so keep it calm and sure, not loud. Slow right down on the four outcomes, use, automate, build, and earn. Do not rush the promise.

## Slide 3 — Course roadmap

> "Here is the whole journey on one screen, so you always know where you are.
>
> We begin with Claude Essentials, the app end to end. Then the 4D Framework, how to work with AI so the results are good enough to depend on. Next, Introduction to Cowork, where you hand whole tasks to Claude. After that, for those who want to build, Claude Code and the Claude API. And we finish with your Capstone Project, turning everything you have learned into real work and income.
>
> One thing to note. Modules one to three, and module six, are for everyone, no coding needed. Modules four and five are the coding modules, and they are optional. Nobody gets left behind, and nobody gets bored.
>
> And the one habit that matters most. Do not just watch. After every lesson, pause, and actually do it yourself. That is how this becomes a skill, not just information.
>
> Let us start at the very beginning. Module one, Claude Essentials."

Delivery notes: point to each module on screen as you name it. Land the "do not just watch" line clearly, it sets the tone for the whole course. End with quiet energy, leading straight into Module 1.

---

## Total run time

About two and a half minutes. Optional hook: before the welcome, hold up something real you built with Claude, or show a finished project for a few seconds, then open with "Assalam o alaikum". Keep the pace unhurried throughout, calm reads as confident.
