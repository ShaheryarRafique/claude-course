# Claude 101 Reference (Anthropic Academy)

Notes scraped from Anthropic's own Claude 101 course, to guide our tone, structure, and content. Their course is the gold standard reference. We adopt the approach, not the words.

## Their Full Course Outline

1. **Meet Claude** — What is Claude? · Your first conversation with Claude · Getting better results · How you'll work with Claude on your desktop
2. **Organizing your work and knowledge** — Introduction to projects · Creating with artifacts · Working with skills
3. **Expanding Claude's reach** — Connecting your tools · Enterprise search · Research for deep dives
4. **Putting it all together** — Claude in action: use-cases by role · Other ways to work with Claude
5. **Conclusion and certificate** — What's next? · Certificate of completion

Takeaway for us: they group lessons into named sections, end with a use-case-by-role lesson and a certificate. Our 8 modules already cover all of this and more (we add Prompt Engineering depth, Claude Code, the API, and an earning focus).

## Their Per-Lesson Structure (adopt this everywhere)

- **Estimated time** (for example, "15 minutes")
- **Learning objectives**: "By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:" followed by action-verb bullets (Start, Write, Upload, Use; Explain, Create, Share; Identify)
- Sometimes a short **Video**
- **Key takeaways**: bold lead-in phrase, then a sentence
- An **interactive moment** (decide for each example)
- **Lesson reflection**: a question that makes the learner apply it to their own work
- **What's next**: a one line bridge to the next lesson

## Tone and Language

- Warm, clear, second person ("you will be able to").
- Reframes rather than lists: "Claude is more than a chatbot, it's a thinking partner."
- "Talk to Claude like you would a coworker: naturally, concisely, conversationally."
- No hype. Honest and practical.

## Key Content Per Lesson (mine this for our modules)

**What is Claude**
- Helpful, honest, and harmless; trained with Constitutional AI to align with human values.
- More than a chatbot: a thinking partner for summarization, search, writing, Q&A, coding.
- Steerable: takes direction on personality, tone, behavior.
- Available everywhere: Free, Pro, Max, Team, Enterprise; syncs across web, desktop, mobile.
- Capabilities: writing and content, research and analysis (200K to 1M token context, about 500+ pages), coding, problem-solving with Thinking (reason step by step), learning mode (guides reasoning instead of giving answers).
- Interfaces: Claude.ai, Claude Code, @Claude in Slack, Claude Design, Claude for Microsoft 365.

**Your first conversation** (maps to our Module 2 and 3)
- Talk like a coworker: naturally, concisely, conversationally.
- Their simple prompt framework: **set the stage** (your role, objectives, context) → **define the task** (the action you want) → **specify rules** (style, tone, examples).
- Upload documents and images to give Claude context.

**Getting better results** (maps to our Module 3)
- Teaches by a troubleshooting table: **Challenge / What's happening / Try this**.
- Example: "Response too generic" → add audience, role, constraints. Before: "Write an email about the project delay." After: "Write an email to our enterprise client explaining the integration will be delayed two weeks. Second delay, keep it professional but apologetic."
- Great device we should copy: a before-and-after prompt table.

**Introduction to projects** (maps to our Module 2)
- Projects are self-contained workspaces with their own memory, chat histories, knowledge bases, and custom instructions. Dedicated environments for a work stream.
- Project knowledge: upload relevant documents so Claude understands your context.

**Creating with artifacts** (maps to our Module 2)
- Content rendered and ready to use: a working website, an interactive chart, a downloadable document, instead of text buried in chat.
- Claude auto-creates an artifact when content is substantial, self-contained, and reusable.
- You can share and publish artifacts.

**Working with skills** (maps to our Module 5)
- Skills are folders of instructions, scripts, and resources that Claude loads dynamically. "Expertise packages" that teach Claude how to do specialized tasks.
- Anthropic ships built-in Skills (for example, document creation).

**Connecting your tools** (maps to our Module 5)
- Connectors bring your tools into the conversation so you don't start from scratch each time.

**Research and Enterprise search** (maps to our Module 5)
- Deep dives across the web and your connected sources.

**Use-cases by role** (maps to our Module 4)
- Practical use cases organized by role so learners see Claude in their own context, each linked to a gallery. "No matter what you do, Claude can help."

## What We Should Apply To Our Course

1. Add **Estimated time** and **Learning objectives** to the start of every module (done for Module 1, do the rest).
2. Use the **"talk like a coworker"** framing and their **set the stage, define the task, specify rules** prompt framework in Module 3, alongside our RTCF template.
3. Add a **before-and-after prompt table** in Module 3 (their strongest teaching device).
4. Tighten our definitions to match theirs: Projects as "self-contained workspaces with their own memory," Artifacts as "rendered, ready to use," Skills as "expertise packages loaded dynamically."
5. Add a **Lesson reflection** question and a **What's next** bridge to each module (done for Module 1).
6. Keep the **use-cases by role** framing in Module 4 (we already name identities).
7. End with a **certificate**, which we already added in Module 8.
