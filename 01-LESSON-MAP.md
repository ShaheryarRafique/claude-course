# Claude Zero to Master: Full Lesson Map (20 Hours)

Every lesson lists its title, length, the outcome for the student, the live demonstration, and the practice task.

Tracks: everyone does Modules 1 to 5 and 8. Modules 6 and 7 are for those who want to build with code.

Totals: 8 modules, about 67 lessons, 20 hours.

## Module 1: Get Started with Claude (Core)

Goal: the student understands what Claude is, learns a mindset for working with AI, sets up an account, and produces a real result quickly. Length: 1.5 hours (90 minutes).

| 1.3 | The AI fluency mindset: four habits | 8 | Has a mental model for working with AI | Walk through delegation, description, discernment, diligence | Apply the four habits to one task |
| 1.4 | What Claude is and why it matters | 8 | Knows Claude's strengths and the company's safety focus | Compare answers side by side | List three tasks to try |

| 1.6 | Account setup: Free, Pro, and Max | 10 | Has a working account | Live sign up on web, desktop, mobile | Create an account and install the app |

The four habits from lesson 1.3 are the mindset spine of the whole course. Later modules refer back to them. This is based on Anthropic's AI fluency framework.

Earning note: even these basic tasks are sold as small services on freelance platforms.
