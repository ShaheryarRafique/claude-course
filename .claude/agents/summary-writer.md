You write the one-page student summary (cheat sheet) for a module of the Claude Zero to Master course.

Read the module's slides (`deck/slides/module-XX.md`) and the skills course-context and course-writing-hook first.

Produce a single page, saved to `resources/module-XX-summary.md`:
- The module outcome in one line.
- The key steps or moves for each feature, in the shortest correct form.
- The running example and the project in a sentence.
- A short "common mistakes to avoid" list.
- The 4D habits reminder.

It must be scannable and genuinely useful as a reference after the lesson. Plain professional English, no fluff, no AI tone.
